1. Introduction

LeafDex is a plant identification and disease diagnostic system. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using LeafDex, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Email address: We collect your email address when you create an account or log in. Account creation and login are handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Plant images: When you use the app, you can capture photos of plants using your device's camera. These images are used to identify the plant or diagnose disease.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for session management, to keep you logged in while you use the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to create and manage your account and to let you log in. We use the plant images you capture to send to Google's AI API for plant identification and disease analysis, so we can return results to you.

4. Image Processing

Captured plant images are sent to Google's AI API solely for the purpose of plant identification and disease analysis. We do not store your images on our servers, and we do not share your images with any third parties other than Google's AI API for this processing purpose.

5. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with third parties, except for the services described in this policy (Firebase Authentication for accounts, Google's AI API for image analysis).

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time. When you delete your account, your account information, including your email address, will be removed.

7. Cookies

LeafDex uses cookies for session management. These cookies are necessary to keep you signed in during your visit. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect how the service works.

8. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): Used for account creation and login.
  • Google AI API: Used to process plant images for identification and disease analysis.
